Vitre-Icons
Original Vitre glass art for the apps that every desktop has: Files, Store, Browser, Settings, Calculator, Terminal, Mail, Calendar,
Music, Photos and about thirty more, plus the start-here logo. ALIASES.txt lists every icon name each piece of art answers to.

Everything it does not draw comes from VitreBase-dark: the glyphs, folders and file types of the MacTahoe icon theme (GPL-3.0, by
Vince Liuice), installed by install.sh as its own copy WITHOUT MacTahoe's app icons. An app that Vitre-Icons has no art for keeps its
own icon.
